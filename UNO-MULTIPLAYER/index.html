How to Play UNO

1

Every player starts with 7 cards.

2

Match the top discard card by Color, Number, or Action symbol.

3

Action Cards: Skip blocks the next player, Reverse changes play direction, and Draw Two forces +2 cards.

4

Wild Cards: Play anytime to pick any color. Wild Draw Four forces +4 cards!

5

Call UNO: Press the UNO button when you have 1 card left. If another player catches you first, you draw 2 penalty cards!

6

First player to get rid of all cards wins!
